ComplyTrade Processing Pipeline

20 steps in 2 phases. Phase 1 runs automatically on upload. Phase 2 starts when you click "Run Verification".

Phase 1 — Document Extraction (Automatic)
1
Raw OCR
Extract text from every page using GLM-OCR
2
OCR Cleaning
Clean and refine extracted text, fix OCR errors
3
Page Sequencing
Group pages into document packets (LC, amendments, shipping docs)
4
MT Identification
Identify SWIFT message types (MT700, MT707, etc.)
5
MT Reconciliation
Match and reconcile multi-page SWIFT messages
6
Final LC Consolidation
Build the Final LC by applying all amendments to the base LC
7
Clause Extraction
Parse F46A (documents required), F47A (conditions), F45A (goods)
8
Shipping Classification
Classify shipping documents (BL, Invoice, Insurance, etc.) using VLM
9
Shipping Reconciliation
VLM-based OCR refinement and field extraction for each document
10
Traceability
Link each document to its source pages and LC clauses
11
Human Review
Flag ambiguous classifications for officer review before verification
Phase 2 — Compliance Verification (On Demand)
12
Clause Decomposition
Break each LC clause into individual checkable conditions via VLM
13
Row Construction
Build the 5-column verification worksheet (blank exam sheet)
14
VLM Verification
Check each condition against actual documents using VLM
14b
Key Term Checks
Verify dates, amounts, ports, shipment deadlines (VLM with rules)
15
Non-Compliance Handling
Classify clauses as checkable, informational, or non-checkable
16
Confidence Review
Escalate low-confidence results to REVIEW for human attention
17
Cross-Clause Dependencies
Resolve F47A overrides on F46A requirements (e.g., Charter Party BL acceptable)
19
Consolidation
Group results into sections for the final report
20
PDF Report
Generate the professional compliance report with cover page and findings

Job Notes

⚠ Delete Job

Job ID:

This will permanently delete the job, all extracted data, and verification results. This action cannot be undone.

⚠ Clear Verification

Job ID:

This will remove only the verification stage outputs (Steps 12–20) for this job.

Kept: Step 01 (OCR), Step 02 (Cleaning), Step 03 (Sequencing), Step 06 (Final LC), Step 07 (Clauses), Step 08 (Classification), Step 09 (Reconciliation).

Removed: Step 12 (Decomposition), Step 13 (Rows), Step 14 (Verification), Step 14b (Implicit), Step 15–Step 20.

After this, you can re-trigger verification from the checklist page — OCR / classification will not re-run, so it will be fast.
